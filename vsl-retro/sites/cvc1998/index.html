About Us

Calgary Vision Centre is a brand new optometry practice in Calgary, Alberta. We opened our doors in 1998.

Our office uses computerised examination equipment. We take the time to explain your eyes to you in plain English, and we never rush an examination.

 Services
  • Complete eye examinations for the whole family
  • Contact lenses, including the new disposable lenses
  • Eyeglasses and a wide selection of frames
  • Computer Vision Syndrome - Do you get headaches or tired eyes at your computer? Ask us about special lenses for monitor use.
  • Laser vision correction consultations and co-management
  • Children's vision and school screening follow-up

Did you know? You can now book your appointment by telephone! Just call (403) 237-0505 during office hours.
